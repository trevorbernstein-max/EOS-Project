Report neighborhood problems. Get them fixed faster.

A community platform that helps residents report issues, notify authorities, and keep everyone informed; all in one website.

Our Problem:

Issues within the neighborhoods usually go unnoticed by authorities. Things like:

  1. Potholes
  2. Broken Streetlamps
  3. Graffiti
  4. Damaged Sidewalks
  5. Noise

But most people dont know:

  1. How to Report it
  2. Who to call
  3. Whether it has been reported already
  4. If the issue is ever resolved

As a result, important problems generally stay unfix for weeks or even months.

Our Solution:

One place to report, track, and share community issues.

The goal is to make communication between residents and local agencies faster, clearer, and more organized.

How it works:

  • Report

    Describe the Issue and upload a picture.

  • Share

    Other residents can view, confirm, and support other submitted reports.

  • Notify

    Authorities are notified of the issue.

  • Track

    Updates are given on the progress until the issue is resolved.

Every report helps better your community.

Whether its a pothole, broken streetlamp, or simple graffiti, your report can make a difference.
