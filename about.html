About this Project

What makes me good for this problem? Why?

Why me?

I started this platform to help communities easily share their local issues, connect with their neighbors, and collaborate on solutions.

Why this problem?

I chose this problem because I have personally experienced this problem. At my house, there are overgrown branches that constantly drop leaves and other debris into my backyard. My mom had tried countless times to get the city to come and cut back these branches, but her attempts were met with negligence.

Where I'm going next.

With this website, I hope to end the negligence of the city, at least the negligence they exhibit when it comes to solving problems within smaller communities.

Every report helps better your community.

Whether its a pothole, broken streetlamp, or simple graffiti, your report can make a difference.

Start reporting today.
