Overgrown Tree Branches

Location: Maple Drive and Pine Street

Reported by: Theresa Reynard

The tree roots are pushing up the sidewalk in a few spots. It's becoming a tripping hazard and could use some attention.

The sidewalk is pretty uneven now from the roots growing underneath. It would be nice to see this fixed for everyone walking through the area.

Just a heads-up that the tree roots have lifted part of the sidewalk. It can be easy to trip if you're not paying attention.
